OUR ORIGIN STORY

Born From Real Family Needs

When our founders tried to coordinate weekly grocery deliveries for their own aging parents, they encountered frustrating hurdles. Mainstream delivery apps rushed couriers, dumped heavy bags at the curb, missed low-sodium and diabetic preferences, and offered no easy tools for family members managing diets from a distance.

We knew there had to be a warmer, safer way. Elderly Eats was born to create a service where senior well-being comes first — with curated nutrition, gentle doorstep handoffs, and clear communication between seniors, drivers, and loved ones.

WHAT DEFINES US

The 4 Pillars of Senior-First Care

Every feature, partnership, and delivery protocol is designed around the needs of older adults and their supporting families.

01

Curated Senior Nutrition

Every grocery item is screened for senior dietary compliance, portion suitability, easy-to-open packaging, and nutritional value.

02

Trained & Patient Drivers

Our couriers understand mobility challenges. They greet recipients warmly, ring the bell, and assist with placing bags on kitchen tables.

03

Caregiver Portal

Family members can order from anywhere, set up auto-replenishment schedules, restrict allergens, and receive instant doorstep photos.

04

Phone-Assisted Orders

For seniors who prefer voice over screens, our friendly care operators take orders over the phone with patience and care.
