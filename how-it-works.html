7 Steps to Peace of Mind

1

Create a Senior Profile

Log in to the Caregiver Dashboard and create profiles for your parents or senior family members. Enter their name, contact, and accurate delivery addresses.

2

Set Dietary Preferences

Check off options like Low Sodium, Gluten-Free, Diabetic-Friendly, or Soft Foods. The system will automatically screen items when shopping to keep it safe.

3

Add Delivery Instructions

Provide unique notes, such as: "Knock loud, mom walks slow," "Please call when at the gate," or "Place bags directly inside the hallway table."

4

Choose Groceries

Select fresh organic vegetables, lactose-free milk, pantry dry goods, paper products, and senior meals. Quantities are easily adjusted.

5

Select Delivery Schedule

Book a preferred delivery date and a comfortable morning or afternoon time slot when they are certain to be awake and home.

6

Track Progress Real-Time

See when the order is packing, dispatched, or arriving. Get tracking updates on the dashboard map or via real-time notifications.

7

Receive Groceries Safely

The delivery captain walks the groceries inside, helps unpack them, and ensures everything is organized. Handoff photo is sent to you instantly.

Vetted With Care

Our Doorstep Safety Protocol

We understand that letting someone into a senior's home requires absolute trust. Our delivery crew isn't rushed. They are paid higher hourly wages to take their time, greet your parent warmly, check that they are doing well, and carefully carry everything inside to the kitchen.

Background Screened

All drivers pass criminal records checks and driver safety vetting.

Senior Sensitivity Training

Drivers learn patience, clear speaking tone, and how to spot safety hazards in senior homes.

Tailored Options

Doorstep Handoff Safe Zones

Specify exactly where our captains should deliver groceries based on accessibility needs.

Standard Counter Setup

Our driver steps inside, carries the heavy bags directly to the kitchen counter, and unpacks the chilled milk or dairy items safely into the refrigerator.

Contactless Secure Box

If the senior prefers privacy or is currently resting, the packages are left inside a customized insulated porch box, followed by SMS photo notification to the child.

Assisted Vestibule Handoff

The delivery captain opens the outer storm door, stacks groceries in the entry vestibule, knocks gently, and steps back to verify that the bags are retrieved.

No Internet Required

Offline Phone Concierge Ordering

We recognize that some senior citizens do not own a smartphone or feel comfortable browsing web menus. Our Care Liaison Hotline bridges this gap. Caregivers can register profiles online, and our operators call the senior on a scheduled weekly hour to verify their needs.

Dedicated Agent Seniors speak to the same warm coordinator every single week.
Dial-In Support Seniors can dial our toll-free number directly to request immediate updates.
