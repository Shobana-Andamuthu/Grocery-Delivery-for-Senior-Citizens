Dietary Security

Our 3-Tier Dietary Security Process

How we guarantee safety and medical compliance for every single ingredient.

Strict Catalog Screening

We cross-check all items against dietary restrictions like low sodium, sugar-free, soft textures, and lactose-free to make sure no invalid item gets ordered.

Care Liaison Approval

A dedicated care manager reviews initial senior profiles and collaborates with family members to tailor their dietary settings perfectly.

Double-Vetted Packing

Our trained warehouse crew manually verifies labels, expiration dates, and packaging integrity before loading them in sanitized coolers.
