Pricing & Fees FAQ

Delivery Support & Fees

No. We do not charge mandatory recurring membership club fees. You only pay for the grocery items you select and the flat delivery fee ($6.99 for one-time, $3.99 for weekly recurring plans). There are no additional packaging or loading fees.
To keep our patient doorstep delivery model sustainable, the minimum grocery order is $25 for one-time deliveries and $20 for weekly recurring deliveries. This ensures we can provide trained drivers who carry groceries inside.
Tipping is completely optional. Our certified delivery captains are paid higher hourly wages specifically to ensure they never rush senior recipients. If you choose to tip, 100% of it goes to the driver.
