Allergen Safety

Allergen Safeguards & Alert Protocol

How we prevent accidental allergen orders with real-time system alerts.

Automatic Cart Bans

Once you configure allergy filters (e.g. peanut or gluten), the shopping cart automatically prevents adding matching items.

Double Vetting Pack Checks

Packers scan barcodes at dispatch. If an item contains a registered allergen, the scanner issues a warning vibration alert.

Special Color Coding

Bags with strict allergy restrictions are packed with bright yellow compliance security ribbons to warn drivers and seniors.

Easy Handling

Lightweight Safety Bags & Counter Packing

Lifting heavy milk jugs or toilet paper packs can cause severe back strain. Our logistics team limits bag weights to a maximum of 8 lbs per bag. Heavy laundry items or liquids are packed separately with dual handle supports. Drivers unpack these bags directly onto kitchen countertops to eliminate all senior bending.

✓ 8 lb maximum weight limit per bag
✓ Dual reinforced handles for easy grip
✓ Countertop unloading and layout check

Vetting Integrity

No-Surprise Replacement Guarantee

If a specific low-sodium soup or whole milk brand is out of stock, we never make automated replacements without consulting you. Our care team calls or texts the caregiver to approve substitutions, ensuring your senior parent never receives a high-sodium or lactose-rich item by mistake.

Diet-Compliant Substitute Guarantee
Strict Standards

Strict Selection Standards for Elderly Safety

We vet every supplier and product before adding them to our system. Fresh fruits must have zero soft spots, dairy items must have at least 10 days of shelf life remaining upon delivery, and ready meals are slow-cooked to assure chewable tenderness.

Sodium Check All sodium-restricted food labels are checked double before packing.
Freshness Checked We never deliver close-to-expiration milk, cheese, or eggs.
